# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed by the user: static HTML5, CSS3, and lightweight vanilla JavaScript only. No framework and no booking backend.

## Users

- Primary users: people in and around München looking for authentic Vietnamese food, often on mobile while deciding where to eat, calling to reserve, or navigating to the restaurant.
- Secondary users: existing guests checking the complete menu, prices, allergens, lunch offer, and opening hours.

## Product Purpose

Present Dong Que Quan München as a real contemporary Vietnamese restaurant, make its full menu easy to browse, and turn interest into a WhatsApp order, WhatsApp reservation, phone call, or route request.

## Positioning

An authentic Vietnamese menu with clearly preserved Vietnamese dish names, broad vegetarian and speciality selections, and concrete local information for the Warngauer Straße restaurant.

## Operating Context

- German-language public restaurant website.
- Most urgent actions are viewing the menu, ordering for pickup or reserving through WhatsApp at +49 89 20569403, checking hours, calling, and opening Google Maps.
- Menu photographs in the project root are the source of truth for dish numbers, names, descriptions, allergen codes, quantities, and prices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page responsive website with anchored navigation.
- Complete menu rendered as searchable/filterable HTML, never as embedded menu photographs.
- Mobile category navigation scrolls horizontally and remains usable while browsing a long menu.
- Every menu item can be added to a persistent browser cart with quantity controls and a calculated subtotal.
- Pickup orders and table reservations create structured WhatsApp requests to +49 89 20569403. The restaurant must confirm each request; no confirmation or payment backend is simulated.
- Phone and route CTAs remain available as direct links.
- Performance-conscious image loading, semantic HTML, keyboard support, reduced-motion support, SEO metadata, OpenGraph, canonical URL, and Restaurant JSON-LD.
- The exact production canonical URL and HoangCaster URL were not supplied and remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dong Que Quan München.
- Voice: natural German, warm, welcoming, concise, and factual.
- Identity: authentic Vietnamese contemporary, elegant, premium, modern, and not Japanese/sushi-coded.
- Binding palette supplied by the user: charcoal, cream/warm white, restrained wine-red accent, muted gold, and green for vegetarian content.
- Binding typography direction supplied by the user: refined serif display face with a clean sans-serif body face.

## Evidence on Hand

- Fifteen unique photographed menu pages plus one duplicate in the project root.
- Restaurant name, address, phone, email, social handles, Google Maps URL/embed, and opening hours supplied in the brief.
- No verified testimonials, awards, founding date, team story, booking service, or analytics events were supplied; these must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Menu accuracy outranks decorative copy.
2. Every featured food image must match the named dish and must not be reused for another dish.
3. Calling, routing, hours, and address must be reachable within seconds on mobile.
4. Vietnamese identity should come from real dishes, language, ingredients, and hospitality rather than generic pan-Asian motifs.
5. Long content must remain calm, legible, and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, accessible menu and lightbox controls, WCAG AA contrast, descriptive German alt text, and a no-motion experience when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled.
