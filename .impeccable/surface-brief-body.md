# Surface brief: index.html

## Scope and mode

Single-page German restaurant site. Mode: Persuade with an Experience-led food narrative.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Guests in München must understand the Vietnamese offer, inspect exact menu details, then call or open a route. Proof comes from the complete photographed menu, precise local information, and dish-specific photography. Preserve all supplied facts, prices, codes, and Vietnamese names. No fabricated booking flow or business history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A lacquered communal table becomes the page itself: dishes arrive as singular, carefully lit moments while the long menu behaves like a calm printed service ledger. Refuse the generic centered restaurant template and ornamental pan-Asian clichés.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black lacquer, warm ivory type, oxblood used only as the decisive accent, muted brass rules, and a restrained herb green for vegetarian content. Sharp editorial picture windows, 12px panels, square-ended rules, and capsule controls only for filtering. A locally served display serif gives hospitality and heritage; a humanist sans carries every operational detail.

STORY: Begin with one arresting Vietnamese dish and the restaurant's exact Munich promise. Move through hospitality, recommendations, the complete filterable menu, four speciality narratives, kitchen glimpses, opening hours, and location. The visitor finishes by calling or planning a route.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed cinematic Phở photograph fills the frame. A narrow translucent-black navigation bar sits at the top. The title occupies the left third, aligned low but fully above the fold; two compact actions sit directly beneath it. Address and hours sit in one quiet bottom rail. The hero image, not decoration, is the memorable device.

FORM: Grounded direction 7, “Lacquered communal table”, seed e011cc60. Signature interaction: the menu category rail locks beneath the header while filtered groups cross-fade without moving the visitor's reading anchor. Motion is one restrained service gesture: hero copy reveals in sequence, then images settle into place on first view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Resolved direction notes

The brief already pinned the Vietnamese-contemporary world, palette, typography, and content hierarchy, so it overrides the concept roll. Catalog challengers were declined on audience identification and product clarity. Their useful disciplines were retained without their visual costumes: one continuous compositional gesture, strict information rhythm, decisive palette commitment, controlled overlap, depth as hierarchy, and stable navigation under change.
